### Playing it on the piano

I was trying to figure out the best way to practice playing this on the piano, and I found a really useful technique that I want to share.

The tricky part of this song is that there is a sequence of notes that is being repeated many times, but some of the notes change. The way I read music, I'm not able to process this well in my brain (it seems like a basic musician skill to be able to do this), so this is how you can practice it:

* Find the first bar where the repeating pattern starts, and set a metronome to some small speed (60bpm is ok).
* Play the pattern with your left hand
* Use a highlighter and highlight the upper notes that are changing
* Try to play only that notes with your right hand (not the whole chord)
* Once you are able to play it, and you are aware of how often the notes are changing, try to "fill in" the rest of the chords with your right hand.

![](https://i.snap.as/GDTAK3T.png)

If you are a musician, this is probably trivial for you, but for me it helped a lot.
