“Ten tabs of acid” is what I called the experience, and it happened in 2018. I have a feeling that my experience was different from others, and I’ve been curious about what other people have experienced on their trips. I’m writing this up in a similar format to [this post](https://www.reddit.com/r/LSD/comments/7m2n5n/my_story_about_10_tabs_of_acid/), as it’s one of the more thorough trip reports I could find.

I don’t remember much about the lead up to the trip, which was in my room. I ran out of water and walked into the kitchen to get some. My dad was standing there. I was surprised by this, and I remember looking at him and thinking “oh, he’s here too.” At that moment, I believed that my dad was just like me and that he’d taken 10 tabs of acid as well. I don’t remember how the conversation went, but I believe I told him this was the case, and he told me it wasn’t, which I found confusing.

I remember sitting on my desk chair, watching something on my computer. My dad had left. I noticed that there was a faint light shining through my window. I looked at it for a while, and then, suddenly, the light exploded. It was incredible, and I was incredibly distracted by it. I remember thinking “oh my god, I’m really high.” The room seemed to fold in on itself, and I realized that I was sitting in my desk chair. I was confused as to how I’d gotten there. I was worried that I’d have to tell my parents I accidentally broke their chair.

I remember realizing that I had to pee, and I tried to get up. I don’t remember trying to get up, but I do remember that I tried and it wasn’t working. I just kept getting dragged down by the chair. I eventually gave up and went back to watching the thing on my computer.

I don’t remember what happened next, but my dad told me that I called him over. He was standing next to me, and he was saying things like “I know you’re coming from a place of love, but you’re being very rude” and “you’ve been ranting, and I’m not sure if you’re aware of this, but I think I’ve had enough.” I don’t remember what I said to him. I remember that I felt very alone. The moment was incredibly isolating.

I don’t remember much about what happened after this. My dad and I were watching a movie in my parents’ room. I was sitting on the couch. I was very anxious and having a hard time focusing on the movie. I eventually got up and walked into my room. My dad told me to go back, but I didn’t. My parents went to my room to get me, and I was hiding under my bed. When they got to me, I saw my mom. My dad had told me that she was having a hard time dealing with this, and I felt very guilty about this.

I don’t remember much about the rest of the night. I remember that my dad and I ended up sleeping in my room together. I was feeling very paranoid and kept getting up to check on my mom. I don’t remember doing this, but my dad told me that I was. He also told me that I kept saying things like “I just don’t know how humans are supposed to do this.”

I woke up the next morning, and my room was a mess. I don’t remember how it got to this state. There were a few things in it that weren’t mine, and I had no idea how they got there.

I don’t remember much about the rest of the day. I’m not sure how long I was tripping. My dad told me that I was still delusional in the morning, but I don’t remember this. I do remember that I was still feeling very strange the day after the trip.

I think this experience taught me a few things. First, it showed me the limits of my mental and emotional stability, and I don’t think I’ll ever be able to forget that. Second, it showed me that I can be very selfish, and I don’t think I’ll ever be able to forget that either.
