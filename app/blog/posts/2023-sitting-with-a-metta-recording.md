I’ve been doing metta (loving kindness meditation) every day since early 2022. In this time, I’ve been using this recording from Tasshin Fogleman.

>“In this practice, we will begin by cultivating lovingkindness for ourselves. And simply by paying attention to the heart and the feeling of the heart we can begin to become more aware of its current condition. It may be a little hard to sense if there is a lot going on in the mind. So see if you can bring your attention to the area of your chest and your heart and just allow yourself to become aware of any feelings or sensations or even images that are arising in this area. Again, allowing yourself to sense the feeling tone of the heart.”

Earlier this year, I was struck by the invitation that this meditation begins with.

> “It may be a little hard to sense if there is a lot going on in the mind. So see if you can bring your attention to the area of your chest and your heart and just allow yourself to become aware of any feelings or sensations or even images that are arising in this area. Again, allowing yourself to sense the feeling tone of the heart.”

This is an important part of the metta meditation that I could sense was being missed. I realized this because when I was going through this meditation, I often struggled to both feel loving kindness towards myself and direct it.

I had to slow down and be with these words because it was what was happening when I was practicing this meditation.

Let’s imagine this vividly.

We are here in the present moment. We are aware that we are in the present moment.

“It may be a little hard to sense if there is a lot going on in the mind.”

Let’s stop.

Let’s look inside our mind. Are thoughts arising? Are they frequent? What are they about? Bring your attention to that.

“So see if you can bring your attention to the area of your chest and your heart”

“Again, allowing yourself to sense the feeling tone of the heart.”

These are two separate invitations. The first is to bring our attention to an area of our body.

The second is to sense the feeling tone of the heart.

When I sat with this, I realized that I was giving myself the metta meditation. I was saying things like “May you be happy, may you be free” and “May you be safe” to me. I was not feeling the heart.

The phrase has a feeling tone. My attention was focused on the phrase, not on the heart.

When I sat with the heart, I noticed that I was feeling a lot of anxiety. When I brought my attention to this, that’s what was there.

I realized that I had been giving myself this phrase/instruction again and again and again and again and again and again and again.

I hadn’t stopped and felt the heart.

That’s what I was missing.

That’s what made me realize that I was just giving myself these phrases. I was not feeling or noticing the feeling tone of the heart.
