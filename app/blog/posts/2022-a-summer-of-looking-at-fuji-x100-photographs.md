I've been interested in getting a Fuji X100 for a while now, so I spent a chunk of last summer just browsing the web for examples of X100 photos. I started with google images, went on to some youtube videos, and spend a couple of hours going through the images at flickr. Here are some of my favorites (feel free to scroll through this section, it's long).

I was surprised by how consistently high quality the images were. I've rarely had a chance to appreciate photography so closely.

These images are particularly interesting to me because the x100 is essentially a 35mm fixed lens camera, and I shoot using a nifty fifty on my T3i. This gives me a very similar perspective, albeit with much less control over the depth of field.

I'm amazed how much the framing in the two images above changes the photo. In both I'm seeing a midshot of a person carrying a biker on their shoulder, but the framing in the first image gives me a sense of scale and location - and the second doesn't.

I'm looking at these photos but I'm still not sure that I'm going to get an X100. So far I've looked but not bought, and I'll see where that takes me.
