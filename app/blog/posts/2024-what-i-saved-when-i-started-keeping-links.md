I started saving links in 2024. My method is to forward any links that I feel are important to a dedicated email address, and then I batch-process those links.

Let's look at some examples of the kinds of things I've saved.

If you go to https://links.sksq.me and hit 'random', you'll get a random entry. Suppose you hit random and picked 7/17/2024. This is what you'd see. 

![image](https://github.com/sksq96/links/assets/46372862/c78f0b2f-6c66-4e7a-8ea1-b8fc20c5e1ed)

To me, this link is interesting because of the clarity and simplicity of the explanation. It's about a topic that is not immediately familiar to me (cryptography). It reminds me of how I can learn about things primarily through analogies to things I already know.

This link is useful because it looks like a good place to find ideas for things to write. It stands out as being a pretty good resource for technical writing.

And this looks like a great starting point for learning about efficient transformers, a topic I want to know more about.

I remember saving these links, and I don't think I've done anything more with them. There are reasons I wanted to know about this. I was thinking about the importance of technical writing. I was interested in cryptography, and transformers.

I'm wondering how often the links I save actually lead to new thoughts/projects/learnings. I'll probably do some more exploring of this kind.
