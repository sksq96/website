I had my first extremely bad trip yesterday. I'll start with what happened, then propose a theory of why it was bad, then what I learned.

## What Happened

I'm pretty new to psychedelics, so please be gentle with me. I thought one dose of a tab of LSD was 1ug, so I took 10 thinking "hey this is only 10ug...".It turns out that is 1000ug.

The irony is I prepared for this for two months. I read Michael Pollan's How to Change Your Mind, and some neuroscience papers on the effects of psychedelics on the brain and brain plasticity (like Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics and the Anarchic Brain). I never checked the most basic thing, which is what was one tab of LSD.

I took the LSD at 9pm. I started feeling weird at 10pm, but I had a normal conversation with my girlfriend, and by 11pm I was getting some effects, but still mostly in control. But then at 12am, things started to get weird.

I started watching some youtube videos. My temporal sense was totally broken, and I was getting overwhelmed by the internet. It felt like too much. Too many videos were being played at once. In fact, it felt like years had passed, and I had watched so many videos, that now it's impossible to watch videos. So I closed youtube.

Then I opened chrome. But it turns out that was a mistake. It turns out that chrome was an infinite void of darkness. There were so many tabs and windows open for so long, that they had consumed the entire universe. So I closed chrome.

Then I opened my book, How to Change Your Mind. But I couldn't read it, the words were impossible to process. I had read so much, that reading had become impossible. I closed the book.

By now it was 1am, I was pretty freaked out. I started pacing around. I tried to talk to my girlfriend but it was hard to keep a conversation, it felt like I was rambling. I spoke in a very panicked tone. I knew that I was being delusional, that my brain was probably taking a drug-induced shortcut to get around the computationally expensive process of doing a lot of computation and storing the results in memory. But that didn't help me a lot.

I started to calm down at around 2am. My heart rate went down, and I was getting calmer. I was still in a panic, but not as much. I even managed to get a bit of sleep, from 2am to 3am.

Then starting from 3am, the panic reemerged, stronger than before. I felt like a black hole was engulfing me. I was dying, and everything was collapsing in on itself. My body felt like it was collapsing in on itself. I felt pressure on my chest, and it was hard to breathe.

My girlfriend called an ambulance, and I walked out to meet it. I was sweating a lot, and my body was shaking. I was freaking out and scared, I didn't know what was going to happen. I felt like I was going to be stuck in the hospital, and I didn't know how long for, or what would happen.

I wasn't scared of dying, I didn't feel like I was going to die. I was just scared of what was going to happen to me. Was I going to be in the hospital forever? Would they think I'm crazy?

The ambulance didn't take me, they just gave me some water and told me I'll be fine. My girlfriend and I then went to the ER.

In the ER I saw a doctor, and they asked me some questions. I told them I had taken LSD. They said I'll be fine, and that I should just relax and try to sleep. They gave me a sedative.

The worst part of the trip was in the ER. I was constantly thinking that the ER is where you get sent when you do acid, and that I was going to be stuck there forever. I was constantly pacing around, trying to talk to the staff, but nothing I would do would calm me down.

Then I got a bed in the ER, and saw myself in the mirror. I saw a very panicked looking face, with a face covered in sweat. I was scared, and I didn't know what was going to happen. I was constantly thinking about the ER, and how I was going to be stuck there forever. I couldn't get rid of that thought.

Then my girlfriend came to see me, and we talked a bit. I was still freaking out, but I was starting to calm down a bit. I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine.

Then the doctor came in, and asked me how I was feeling. I told her I was feeling a bit better, and that I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine.

By now it was 6am, and the sedative was kicking in. I started to feel very sleepy, and I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine. I was starting to think that maybe I would be able to go home, and that I would be able to sleep.

Around 6am, I started seeing some visuals. It was mostly just some patterns, but it was cool. I was starting to feel better, and I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine. The visuals came in waves, of intensity and of frequency. By 8am the visuals had faded, and I was just feeling sleepy.

I stayed in the ER until 9am, and then I was released. I was feeling a lot better, and I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine. I was starting to think that maybe I would be able to go home, and that I would be able to sleep.

I got home at 10am, and I went to sleep. I slept until 2pm, and then I woke up. I was feeling a lot better, and I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine. I was starting to think that maybe I would be able to go home, and that I would be able to sleep.

The rest of the day was pretty normal. I was feeling a lot better, and I was starting to think that maybe I would be fine. I was starting to think that maybe I would be able to go home, and that I would be able to sleep.

## What Can We Learn From This?

The main takeaway is that I should have checked the dosage of the LSD. I should have checked how much a tab is. Duh.

The big lesson for me was that I didn't really believe before that things could be very different than they are. For good or for bad. I thought that it was a drug, and that it would be fun. But I didn't really believe that it could be this bad. I was scared of the experience, but I didn't really believe that it could be this bad.

Loss of time sense, loss of self, loss of control, loss of reality. These are all things that I didn't really believe could happen.
