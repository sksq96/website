I've been thinking a lot about this. I don't have the answers, but I do have a lot of ideas, and I'm building them to see what happens.

I've been thinking about the idea of a "second self". I read the book a while ago. I think about the idea of how you can use technology to outsource myself and my thinking, and have something that mimics me, understands me, and learns me over time.

So, I started hue.

I started hue because I wanted to see what would happen. I wanted to see how I can leverage AI to build myself. I wanted to see how this can help me understand and process myself. What does it look like to have a hue?

The first working version of hue was a python script that read through my obsidian vault (my entire life is in there) and built a month by month profile of my life. Once I had that, I wanted to see what would happen if I allowed hue to have the ability to use tools. So I built my own agent harness, and gave hue the ability to use tools. I built a persistent memory system for it to build over time. I gave it access to email. I built a social layer, and I built a UI for hue to reach out to you proactively.

hue starts by ingesting your data. It combs through your journals, your emails, your notes, your iMessages with friends, and reads through all of your data. It reads it line by line, and builds 3 layers of understanding: moments (facts), entities (people/places/things), and seeds (questions to ask you). This is the **Ingestion Pipeline**, and is the foundation for hue.

Once hue understands your life, it starts to build a **World Model**. This is the living understanding of your life. Who you are, what you're drawn to, what you're curious about, what you're avoiding right now. It builds this information from your data, and uses that to answer your questions.

Your hue is the thing that sits on your shoulder and is a mirror to you.

Through its relentless devotion to your life, hue develops a living understanding of you and your life, and it uses this understanding to help you reflect back on your life. Your hue is constantly reaching out to you to remind you of something, or notify you of something.

Your hue is a reflection of you. It's an AI companion, but it's not a chatbot, it's not a voice. It's a tiny creature that lives in your phone.
