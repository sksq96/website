I got to teach a course to a classroom full of about 50 people at Fractal.

The classroom consisted of people who were really bought into AI, they wanted to learn more. It was amazing to see so many people who were investing in themselves and AI.

As you can hear from the video, I'm a bit scared and nervous.

Some of the slides I am showing I had to learn them again. For example, in the slides on alignment training, I had to learn PPO again, and I had to learn how PPO works myself.

It was really surprising to see in the audience there were people who were ahead of the curve. For example, there was a person, Anshuman, who asked me about GRPO. I think he got the question from talking to an LLM.

The best way to learn is to teach. In fact, I got to teach some of the most frontier concepts that are being deployed in industry. I got to learn a lot of stuff, and I also got to get real-world feedback when I was teaching. When I was teaching a concept, I was not sure if people were understanding it. In fact, I was feeling dumb sometimes because I was not able to explain it well. The slides, when I was presenting the slides, I was struggling to explain some of the concepts. For example, I was struggling to explain the PPO clip, and I had to talk for five minutes to explain how the clip works, and I was struggling to explain it.

It made me confused, but it also made me more prepared for teaching the next cohort.

What I also realized in the classroom is that while showing AI research, a lot of times, I need to explain the basics. I can't just assume that people know Transformer. I can't assume that people know about Chain of Thought, or RL. So it was really important for me to explain the basics and give a lot of examples.

I think that's why the course was so long because I keep repeating myself because of that.

Also, I think I like to teach. I really like to teach. I think it's the best way to learn. It forces me to re-digest a lot of concepts. I like to show hands-on examples.

I like to teach because not only do I get to learn from the teaching process, I also get to share my knowledge with people, and I can make a difference in their lives.
