You sit in a beach bar under a sky of pink and yellow. You’re drinking a club soda with a slice of lime and mint leaves. 

That’s the first thing I remember, when I think about the time when I started falling in love with someone, before we even started dating.

I can’t remember the date, but I can look up a picture on my phone of her, laughing, under a colorful roof. I can look up the place, and find the website that gives the opening hours, and a map to get to the place. I could even find out what the weather was like that day.

That would certainly give me more information about the situation at the moment. And who knows, maybe that would change the way I remember that moment.

Now I know that the weather that day was 70 degrees Fahrenheit and wind speed 3.1 mph, and that the sunset was at 7:48 PM. But that doesn’t change much about the scene in my memory. I still remember drinking club soda and looking at her, when she was laughing.

Now, what about the words that were said? My memory shows someone saying: “So they were cut off!” But who said that? A woman said it, or maybe a man. That’s as far as my memory reaches. But I can dig deeper, and look up my messages with her around that time. And sometimes I find out exactly who said what.

I have no idea what we were talking about when we were in that beach bar. I can’t remember. But I can look up the messages. And I see that she told me that my uncle *converted*, in the context of speaking about my uncle, who is part of a new religious movement.

And I remember that. I do. I remember being annoyed at the use of the word *converted*. I remember that I was annoyed at the word, but not at her. And I remember that in my memory the message is some version of *“So they were cut off”*.

This is what I mean with the question, *which parts do I remember?* I can remember part of a scene, and not being able to remember the exact words that were said. Even though I can do a search on my phone and find the exact words.

And here we get to the interesting stuff. Because I do remember my uncle and the use of the word *converted*. But that’s not what my memory has stored in connection to the beach bar scene.

So yeah, there is a discrepancy between what my memory has stored and what can be corroborated by pictures and messages. I don’t know what the right interpretation is here. It might be that my memory inserted the wrong message in the wrong place.

It might also be that there was a moment where we were talking about my uncle, and a completely different moment where someone said *“So they were cut off”.* And my memory just merged them together.

But maybe not. Maybe it is the case that in my memory, the beach bar scene is associated with the use of the word *converted*. And maybe my memory is trying to tell me something here.

I don’t know. It could be that my memory stored the wrong thing. Or it could be that my memory has stored the right thing, and that this discrepancy between the scene and the message is exactly what my memory wants to tell me.

And I remember the beach bar part. It’s here for me to see. I also remember my annoyance at the use of the word *converted*. It’s there for me to see. But the exact details of who said what when, that’s something I could look up in my messages. And I’m not sure it would add anything to my understanding of what it was like for me back then.
