## What is interoception?

Your brain is continuously monitoring signals from inside your body: your heartbeat, breathing, gut tension, temperature, muscle state, energy level. Interoception is your awareness of those signals.

For example, if I'm aware that my heart is beating fast, that's interoception.

I'm intrigued by this because interoception can help improve self-regulation. Also, I think it's easy to overlook. For instance, when I'm feeling anxious, I can perhaps label that as "feeling tense" and then try to calm down. But what does that tension feel like?

I think it's easy to take a high-level, top-down approach to emotions and cognition, but I want to improve my ability to take a bottom-up approach too.

Interoception is hard. You need to pay attention to your body, and it's hard to do that.

One thing I find challenging about interoception is that it's so... subjective. When I think about my thoughts, I can at least try to be objective about my thoughts, but it's hard to be objective about my feelings.
