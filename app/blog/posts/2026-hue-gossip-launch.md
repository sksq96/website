I made videos for [Hue](https://hue.gossip.app), an AI you text in iMessage. The videos took a lot of time and generations to make.

ChatGPT and I made the plans for the video. The characters were generated through Nano Banana, the scenes were generated through Seedance 2.5. I would double check the scenes, and if they were sloppy, I would ask Seedance to fix it and regenerate the scene. And this was a very long process, but that's exactly what I wanted to experience. I wanted to see what Claude's capability was in video generation.

I learned a lot from this. I learned that video generation is not what I expected. I expected that we could generate the video in one pass, but it seems like I couldn't. The video generation models are not smart enough to generate the video correctly in one-shot. I found that it takes many, many attempts to generate videos using AI. It will never be one-shot. Something will always go wrong.

I also learned that using different platforms can result in different videos. Seedance 2.5 through Kie.ai is different from Seedance 2.5 that Seedance built themselves. Seedance 2.5 is also different from Kling. They all have their own strengths and weaknesses.

I also learned that AI will always try to gaslight you. It will always try to convince you that this is the best video and that you should move on to the next scene. But if you actually look at the video, you would realize that it's actually very bad. So AI will always try to convince you that its work is good, and we ought to look at it with a very critical eye.

I also learned that Remotion is great for editing.

I got Hue: Buzz, and Hue: Anime. Now I'm going to add These videos to the landing page.

I also asked Claude to save the pipeline and the recipes in a GitHub repository: [sksq96/hue-gossip-launch](https://github.com/sksq96/hue-gossip-launch)
