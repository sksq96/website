I have a new favorite prompt. I used it to make this image. Look how cute the pup looks!

"A dachshund dog wearing a jean jacket, standing in a meadow"

It was very hard to get a good generation for this. I had to try more than 15 times before I got something that I liked. I'm not sure why it was so hard, but I think this is a case of the model having to learn a new distribution for all the prompts out there. The model is probably not used to generating images with a dog in jackets. I think it's a matter of time before the model gets better at this and my prompt will work consistently.

Today I want to talk about how to create good prompts for clothing on pets. My focus here will be on dogs because dogs are more expressive than cats or any other pets.

First let's talk about the prompts that are not good.

If you use the prompts like these, you will get results that are not so good. The clothing will look unnatural on the pets.

"A dog wearing a jacket"

"A dog in a jacket"

"A jacket on dog"

"Dog in a jacket"

"Dog with a jacket on"

These are bad prompt because they don't specify where on the dog the clothing should be.

So, what are the prompts that are good?

The good prompts are the ones that specify where on the dog the clothing should be.

"A dog wearing a jacket on its body"

"A dog in a jacket on his body"

"A jacket on dog's body"

"Dog in a jacket on his body"

"Dog with a jacket on his body"

And here is my favorite prompt:

"A dog wearing a jacket on his body, standing in a meadow"

I like it because it gives the dog a place to be. It's not just a dog in a jacket, it's a dog in a jacket standing in a meadow. I think it makes the image more interesting.

Here is the prompt for the wizard outfit. I used this to create the image above.

"A dog wearing a wizard hat and robe. The robe has a star pattern on it. The background is a deep space scene with stars and galaxies."

I like it because it's very specific. It tells the model exactly what to do. It also gives the model some freedom to create. The model can choose the color of the robe and the hat. It can also choose the color of the stars and galaxies.

I have to admit that I'm not an expert in prompts. I'm still learning. I'm sure there are better prompts out there. If you have any suggestions, please let me know.

Here's what I know. I like prompts that have these properties:

- They are specific. They tell the model exactly what to do.

- They are not too specific. They give the model some freedom to create.

- They are interesting. They make the image more interesting.

- They are fun. They make me smile.

- They are simple. They are easy to understand.
