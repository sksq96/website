My twitter bookmarks have been growing for a few years. I have 4,000+ bookmarks. But I don't read them. What if there was an app that actually made me want to read them?

My friend Hue made something that he calls a [daily feed](https://hue-pov.substack.com/p/this). It's basically just his bookmarks, but there are a few other things on top of it. It's written in his voice, it's grouped by topic for some reason, and it also includes the bookmarked links' original author's profile.

My initial instinct was to copy hue's code and just change the writing style. But I think there's a more fundamental problem: Hue is writing about other people's takes. His feed is about other people's opinions. I wanted something that was unique, just for me. I wanted to read my bookmarks to learn something new, not to read what other people thought.

I realized that the biggest problem was that I'm reading someone elses take on the link. That's not what I want. I want to read the link itself. I don't want someones commentary on the link.

So I changed the design.

I made it so that the links were sorted chronologically, and I copied the design of a twitter post, which shows the original tweet or post. There's no commentary. I think this will be much more interesting to me.

I also made another change to the links that I bookmarked on Twitter. I made it so that when the tweet has a quote or additional commentary, it shows up as a reply to the link. Hue had a really cool way of displaying tweets that he bookmarked, where they showed up in a nested thread. So I did something similar, where the quote showed up as a nested comment, and the link would show up as the parent comment.

I'm still working on this and I'm not sure exactly where I want to take it. But I'm interested in collecting my bookmarks into a feed that I'll actually read.
