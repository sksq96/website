twitter is my main source of the people i value, and my main way of talking to them too. i've had this itch for a tool that would do a better job than twitter for the usecases i care about.

i think that twitter is a good tool for people who are extroverts, or who have a big social network, or who offload a lot of their reasoning to others, or who are looking to be inspired.

but i think it's not a great tool for me personally. i am an introvert. i have a small social network. i like to do my own reasoning. and i'm looking to be productive, not inspired. i vibecoded a prototype of a tool that i think does a better job than twitter for my usecases.

the way it works is that it just takes a list of accounts that i think are the best of the best, and it only shows me their tweets and only lets me reply to their tweets. it's like twitter, but only the best of the best.

you can try it at: [console.sksq96.com](https://console.sksq96.com)

if you have any other ideas on how to improve it, please let me know. i'm always looking for ways to improve my tools.

## Architecture

the architecture is really simple. just a vercel app. i used [brainstorms](/brainstorms) to get the list of accounts that i think are the best of the best, and then i just used that list to filter the tweets that i see and the replies that i can make.

[brainstorms](/brainstorms) is my private repo where i keep all my ideas and notes and thoughts and reasoning and plans and drafts and everything else that i think is important and i want to keep track of.
