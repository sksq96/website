I've been messing around with Twitter for a while, and I think I'm starting to get the hang of it. Twitter is somewhat complex as a platform, but I've been interested in posting more and having good posts. So I thought to myself, why not try to make an automated twitter post thing?

I'm dumb so it took me a while to get a decently working one...

So anyway. I have a working UI that lets me look at my interests, the Tweets that I've saved, and the tweets I want to make.

![](images/twitter-voice/1.png)

I have been spending a lot of time setting up my second brain and obsidian. Codex is a smart fella and he figured out I had saved links and figured out I was interested in certain things.

![](images/twitter-voice/2.png)

I then started looking at my tweets. My tweets are trash...

![](images/twitter-voice/3.png)

I'd like to use Twitter as a way to meet like-minded individuals, and so I am trying to be more active. Twitter is a fun platform.

![](images/twitter-voice/4.png)

Ok, so I have a tool that tells me what I'm interested in. I also have my Twitter account. So I think to myself, what can I do with this?

Well, since I already have a lot of information, I could just use it to write tweets for me.

So I start having Codex browse through Twitter to understand what's trending - and then I have it start writing for me.

![](images/twitter-voice/5.png)

I have this skill called `twitter-voice` and that is supposed to look at my previous writing and try to write in a way that I'd like.

I then have it look at what's trending and the things that I'm interested in and see if it can write something that I'd like to tweet.

![](images/twitter-voice/6.png)

I also have it show me a preview of what the tweet will look like, so that I can see it in light mode and dark mode.
