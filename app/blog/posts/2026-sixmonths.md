An experiment in intentionally living six months in the future. Inspired by Dario Amodei and a tweet from Dan.

The idea: I've been given a window to the future. I can do what I like in it. But I'll need to report back to the present and prepare for what is to come. What would I do?

I have 183 days.

I'll write more about this soon.

## Getting started

*The very beginning.*

**Sept 16** - I'm starting this at 00:31 on Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

**Sept 17** - I started sixmonths this last Tuesday. I was inspired to start it after seeing @darioamodei's article on [Machines of Loving Grace](https://darioamodei.com/essay/machines-of-loving-grace) and a tweet from @d4n. I was fascinated by the idea of having a glimpse of the future. What would I do differently with that glimpse? 

Inspired by their writing, I'm going to be attempting to write about my own version of the future.

I'm going to be starting this sixmonths experiment on September 16, 2026 and planning to end it on March 16, 2027. That means I'll get to publish this post on my blog exactly six months from the starting date.

As I'm starting this, I'm creating an agenda for my first week. I'm going to start with the basics of creating a strong foundation for writing and thinking about sixmonths.

Some of my first week's to-do list:
- Setup the project with the right templates
- Think about what I want to write about
- Disconnect from social media and go on a 3-day Twitter hiatus
- Read some books I've been wanting to read
- Setup a good research process
- Shift my priorities away from work and towards writing

Over the next few days, I'll be coming back with more thoughts and updates from the start of the sixmonths experiment.

## Telling my story

*Why I wanted to do this and how I'm planning to tell the story of sixmonths.*

**Oct 15** - I'm starting to think about how I want to tell the story of sixmonths. I've been doing a lot of research and reading, but I haven't been writing that much. I've been sharing some updates on Twitter, but I haven't been sharing a lot of the process behind it. I'm starting to think that I should be writing more and sharing more about the process. I'm going to be telling my story.

### Why I wanted to do this

When I first read Dario's writing on Machines of Loving Grace, I was inspired by the idea of having a glimpse of the future. I thought it was a fascinating idea, and I wanted to explore it further. I also saw Dan's tweet about his own sixmonths, and I was inspired by his writing and the idea of writing about the future.

I thought this would be a fun and interesting project to do. I'm always interested in the future, and I like to think about what the future might look like. I've been wanting to write more, and I thought this would be a good way to motivate myself to write more. I've also been wanting to do more research, and I thought this would be a good way to motivate myself to do more research.

I also wanted to do this because I'm interested in the idea of time travel. I don't think time travel is possible, but I do think it's a fun idea to explore. I've always been fascinated by the idea of time travel, and I thought this would be a good way to explore it further.

### How I'm planning to tell the story

I'm planning to tell the story of sixmonths by writing about the process and sharing updates along the way. I'm going to be writing about the research I'm doing, the books I'm reading, and the thoughts I'm having. I'm going to be sharing updates on Twitter, and I'm going to be sharing updates on my blog. I'm also planning to share some of the artifacts I create along the way, such as the research reports and the posts I write. I'm not sure yet exactly what I'll be sharing, but I'm planning to share a lot of it. I'm excited to see how it all comes together!
