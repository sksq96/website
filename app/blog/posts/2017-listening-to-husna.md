My first exposure to the Coke Studio Series was with **Husna**. The one I watched was from **June 2017**.

I don't know who the lead guy is, but his voice is amazing. The music is soothing yet at the same time makes me anxious to listen to the next line in the song.

The opening line I particularly like is:

"Tum ho to har ik lamha muqaddas ho jaye"

The harmonium (?) playing in the background is soothing but at the same time anxious. I can't really describe it. It alters after "Tum kahti ho achha ji main chalta hoon". It makes me want to replay that bit of the song over and over again. I like the way the harmonium enters and lingers for a bit in the background. The alteration is the best part I like about the song. There is a melancholic feel to the whole song.

The song is supposed to be about a lover telling Husna he is leaving while it is still daylight while she fears the possibility of him not returning. Shows you what the powers of love can do. You can build the entire plot of the song just by listening to it.

Since I had no idea what the song was really about, I understand some parts of it. I also made up my own story for it. But that's the beauty of listening to a story in a language you don't understand. It builds your own imagination of the story.

I watched this again in **November 2017**. I wanted to link the song again, but I couldn't find it. I asked a friend, and she found it for me. She could not find it because, in my first draft, I had the wrong names for the artist.

The next day, I was at the doctor's waiting for my turn. I started listening to the song and I was lost in it. The wait was over before the song finished.

I like it even more on the second listen, but there is a small chance I might like the song less after the next few listens. But when I do, I'll probably discover another song I love.

**April 2018**

I'm listening to Husna again. I found it while trying to listen to Asad Khan's "Main Kyu Na". It's been a while since I've listened to it last. I remember enjoying it, but I don't remember why. I remember the first line, so that's a start. While listening to it, I happened to read the comments on YouTube, and one stuck out.

"This is probably one of the most beautiful songs I've ever heard."

I agree. It touches a part of my soul. The part I want to be touched the most.

**July 2018**

I listened to Husna last week. I like it a lot!
