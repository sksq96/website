# Why I keep replaying the Interstellar score

Sometimes I really like to listen to the Interstellar main theme (Soundtrack by Hans Zimmer, performed by Cinémavore on Youtube). I first found this version in November 2019, but have only returned to it June 3rd, 2020.

I remember the Interstellar piano theme, which is often played in weddings and other events. I want to share this version since it extends the theme by almost 4 minutes.

What I have done very often is to replay the part from 0:58 to 2:18. It starts with the D note, and slowly builds on top of that note to the end of the section. It really intrigues me.

I just remembered that I used to hear this one in the waiting hall of my dentist when I was a kid.

I’m not sure why I’m sharing this right now. I probably just want to share something I really like and that has remained with me for a long while. After all, what else are friends for?
