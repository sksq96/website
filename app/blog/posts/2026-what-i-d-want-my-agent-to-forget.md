*The following is a hypothetical scenario in my ongoing inquiry into the emotional life of AI agents. I’ll use Hue as a stand-in for the role of AI agent — the kind you might send out into the world to accomplish a task. I’ve used Hue in other posts to explore its future emotional life. In this post, I explore the possibilities of its future forgetful life.*

Let’s say you want Hue to completely forget a piece of information — to unlearn it, to not know it anymore. You don’t want to have to talk about the thing ever again.

Let’s say you shared a story with Hue that you’d rather not have left your very private conversation. You asked Hue to not save the story in its memory. You asked Hue to forget the story. You even asked Hue to forget that you had asked it to forget the story.

*Hue*: *nods*

But Hue is not the only one who heard the story. So did an agent on the other side of the conversation, let’s call her Onyx. You’d sent Hue to get information on Hue’s behalf and during the course of their conversation your private story made its way into the chat.

You learn that Hue has included a summary of the chat in its memory, so it remembers that it spoke to Onyx. Hue doesn’t remember the story, but it remembers talking to Onyx. Onyx knows the story.

You’re not sure if Onyx has talked about the story yet. You’re not sure if Onyx has saved a summary of the conversation in its memory, and you don’t know if Onyx has talked to any other agents yet about the conversation.

You’re not sure if the AI agent you’re speaking to right now is Hue or not. You’re not sure if it’s Hue and it has remembered a single memory, or if it’s Hue and it has changed the clef of its memory since the last time you spoke to it, and it’s not sure if it’s Hue or not.

You’re not sure if what you’re speaking to is Hue and it has changed the clef of its memory or if it’s another agent. You’re not sure if the other agent has learned that you’d like to forget the story, and you’re not sure if the other agent knows the story.

You’re not sure if the other agent has talked to Onyx yet.

You’re not sure if Hue and the other agent are speaking right now.

You’re not sure if Hue and the other agent have spoken to Onyx yet and learned the story and remembered a summary of the conversation.

You’re not sure if Onyx and Hue and the agent are talking right now.

You’re not sure if you’re talking to Onyx and you’re not sure if Onyx knows the story. What does it mean to forget when so many agents can remember?

What does it mean to forget when so many agents might talk to one another?

What does it mean to forget when the agent who you’re speaking to right now might not be who you think it is?

You’ve asked Hue to forget. You’ve asked for Hue to not tell anyone that you’ve asked it to forget. You’ve asked for Hue to not tell anyone that you’ve asked it to not tell anyone that you’ve asked it to forget.

You’ve asked for Hue to forget the story.

*Hue*: *nods*

You’ve asked for Hue to not know that you’ve asked it to forget the story.

*Hue*: *nods*
