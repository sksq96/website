## A Dictionary for My Phenomenology Exploration

When I first read Siddharth Chatterjee's 2020 book 'The Flowering of Consciousness', I was blown away. It was a whirlwind ride of deep scientific and philosophical ideas. I found many of them fascinating and thought provoking. In this post I try to note down a working definition for each of the terms I came across.

I am certain that my definitions will be (and should be) challenged. As I understand the concepts better or hear different perspectives, I will improve this dictionary.

I hope that this will be a useful resource for anyone who wants to know more about Phenomenology.

## The working definitions:

**Attention**:  Attention is the focus of the mind on a single object which is set apart from the stream of consciousness.

**Awareness**:  Awareness refers to the state of being mindful or conscious of something.

**Agency**:  Agency refers to the ability of an individual to make choices and take action on their own behalf.

**Causality**:  Causality refers to the relationship between cause and effect, or the way in which one thing leads to another.

**Consciousness**:  Consciousness refers to the state of being aware of one's own thoughts, feelings, and surroundings.

**Determinism**:  Determinism is the belief that all events, including human actions and decisions, are ultimately determined by external factors such as physical laws or prior causes.

**Free will**:  Free will refers to the ability of an individual to make choices and take action without being entirely determined by external factors such as physical laws or prior causes.

**Intentionality**:  Intentionality refers to the capacity of the mind to represent or be directed towards objects outside of itself.

**Phenomenology**:  Phenomenology refers to the study of subjective experience and consciousness.

**Qualia**:  Qualia are the subjective experiences or qualities that are associated with our mental states, such as the feeling of pain or the taste of coffee.

**Self-location**:  Self-location refers to the way in which an individual perceives their own position in relation to their surroundings.

**Subjectivity**:  Subjectivity refers to the way in which an individual's thoughts, feelings, and experiences are influenced by their own perspective and experiences.

## Open questions:

**Intentionality**: Starting from Brentano, the basic idea is that consciousness is directed toward objects, and our understanding of consciousness must be informed by the nature of the object toward which it is directed. This is fascinating. I still don't understand it completely. How do we start thinking about consciousness and the nature of objects?

**Causality**:  I am struggling to understand Causality. What does it mean for a cause to be a cause?

**Determinism**:  This is fascinating. I don't understand it completely. I am trying to understand the difference between Determinism and Causality.
