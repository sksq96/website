I made this picture of my dog Bunny for a story I was writing.  He's a siberian husky and a very good boy!  It was pretty fun to make.  Making a 3D model for him was very difficult, but with some photo manipulation and a lot of prompting I was able to get something that looks like him.

The basic 3D model was made with Meshy.ai which is a super cool new tool that I hadn't played with before!  Here is a link to the model:

Some other tools I used:

- ChatGPT: this was used to generate all of the images that I needed to make anything, except for a few of the grass textures that I took from online and edited
- Blender: Blender is mostly overkill for what I needed here, but I used it to add the models, textures, and camera to the scene and to render it
- GIMP: I used GIMP to edit the images that I used as textures or alpha cutouts to mimic the grass in the park

I worked through a few different iterations of this, using different angles, camera lenses, render settings, and lighting to get different results.  The final image was rendered with Eevee, and I spent a lot of time fiddling with the shadows and the ambient lighting.  I got some good results with Cycles, but the most accurate render wasn't the most pleasing.  Eevee was better at getting a uniform illumination and an "outdoors" feel.  I have basically no experience with Blender, so if you have a better idea how to get a nice effect please comment and let me know!

# Where is Bunny

In the park it's easy to find Bunny, just look for the siberian husky in the middle of the frame!  In Blender, not so much.  It took me a while to figure out how to navigate around the model, so here is a quick guide.

The first thing to do is change your view to the camera view.  This is what you will use to find Bunny.  Click on View -> Cameras -> Active Camera (or press CTRL+0).  Then you will be looking at the scene through the camera's eyes.  From there, if you want to find Bunny, look at the outliner in the top right.  If you don't see Bunny in the outliner, click on the circle to the right of "Scene Collection" to expand the view.  Then you can click "Bunny" and you will see him highlighted in the scene.
