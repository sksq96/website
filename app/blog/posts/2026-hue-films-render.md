This is my tessaract.js skill repo, from Sep 2026. The goal was to generate vertical videos, like those iPhone mockups that do ad for apps.

The videos are pretty cute. This is the best one, IMO.

It was one of my first skills. It was quite different from others. I knew how to build skills, and I knew how to create videos. But I had zero idea about how to render video frames in HTML, even though I had built videos in HTML. But tessaract.js does it in a very different way. So it was a big learning curve for me to understand how to do it properly.

I had the idea along with my co-founders, of how it should be. But I didn't know what was needed, and I didn't know the right questions to ask. During the first phase of the project, the framing was slightly wrong. I thought I need to build the iPhone app mockup components, and run the same film scripts through different components to get different variations. But that was a mistake. I ended up building 20+ components, but I barely used them. Instead, I ended up rebuilding the components in the film scripts themselves.
