I have a friend who occasionally has synesthesia and a couple of weeks ago he posted a video that was supposed to induce it. It was made by a guy called Warren Neidich who is a neuroscientist and artist. These are the turns it attempts to induce.

This video which is a recreation of the original, is to be viewed on full screen in a dark environment with headphones. I tried it twice. The first time I got nothing. The second time I got a few seconds of the first section, but it was not very remarkable. Here I will say some of the things that I thought were going on, but bear in mind a couple of things. Firstly, if I did not really experience anything then I am just spitballing. Secondly, a lot of this will be hard to see without watching the video.

It is important to understand that all of these things are happening at the same time. The sound is co-ordinated with the colors and with the movements.

**What is happening?**

Let’s pause the video at 0:58 and talk about what is happening there.

**Sound:** There is a tone that is oscillating at two or three different frequencies. If I were to rate it on a musical scale, I would say it is a relatively high note.

**Color:** There are two colors at that moment, green and a sort of white, but shaded slightly orange.

**Movements:** That is not the right word, because what we are looking at does not really move, it is more like a still image. So what changes is something else. What changes are these things I will call “attributes”. One of the attributes is the size of the green circle. The green circle is pulsing. It is getting bigger and smaller. Another attribute is the position of the white lines and the little white dots. They are pulsing too. It seems like they are positioned in a wave-like pattern, and it seems like the wave is moving towards you or away from you depending on where you are in the cycle.

**Color again:** At this point in the video, the colors are changing too. The background is changing from green to white. The green circle is doing the same. At first glance the image is different every time the colors change, but on closer inspection it is the same shape, just the colors have been inverted. That is, when the background is green, the circle is white, and when the background is white, the circle is green.

Now, it seems like the green and the white have different effects on the viewer. It seems like the green is more relaxing and the white is more stimulating.

**More sound:** So the sound is the same for both the green and the white, but the sound and the colors are co-ordinated. The sound is the same but the pitch changes. Specifically, the pitch changes with the colors. The high pitch is played when the colors are white and the low pitch is played when the colors are green.

**Timing:** The colors are changing, the circles and lines are pulsing, and the sound is changing. All of these things are happening at the same time. At this point in the video, they are all happening at the same time. That is important. It is not like the sound is playing and then the colors change and then the circle pulses. They are all happening at the same time. They are all co-ordinated.

**Takeaways:** So what is the purpose of this? If you are having this experience and you are focusing on the sound and focusing on what you are seeing, what is happening to you? I am not sure, but here are some thoughts.

**Relaxation, tension, and fear:** The green is accompanied by a lower pitch and the lower pitch is more relaxing. The white is accompanied by a higher pitch and the higher pitch is more stimulating.

**Visual hallucinations:** When the higher pitch is playing, the green circle is white. The area between the green circle and the edge of the screen is green. It is all the same shade of green. But when you stare at it for a moment, it starts to look like there are other shades of green in there. It looks almost 3D. I do not know why this happens or what it means, but it is interesting.

**Music:** I know next to nothing about music, but if I had to guess, I would say that the sound is like a throbbing baseline. I am not sure if that is the right word. It is a high note played on a low instrument. I think most people would agree that it is very hard to dance to. It is not my personal taste but you can sort of see what someone like that is going for.

**Synesthesia:** So what is happening with the synesthesia? Because it is happening or not happening in your head, you can tell yourself that it is happening or not happening. You can tell yourself that the sound is making you see colors or that the colors are making you hear sounds. You can tell yourself that the sound is making you feel relaxed or that the colors are making you feel stimulated. You can tell yourself that the sound is making you feel happy or that the colors are making you feel sad. You can tell yourself that the sound is making you feel like you are in a different place or that the colors are making you feel like you are in a different time.

All of these things are happening in your head, but are any of them synesthesia? I do not know. I am not sure if I have ever had synesthesia. I have had experiences where I have felt like I have had synesthesia, but I do not know if that is what synesthesia is.

**Other notes**

So that is everything I can say about the first minute of the video. I will end with a couple of final points.

**Can you see the synesthesia?**

You can see that the colors are changing, that the circles and lines are pulsing, and that the sound is changing. All of these things are happening at the same time. But can you see the synesthesia?
