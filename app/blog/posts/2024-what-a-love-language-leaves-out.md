*2024, 12/12/23 - 12/18/23*

One of the activities I’m doing this month (October) is a love language quiz, which is a way for people to figure out how they best give and receive affection. It’s an interesting exercise because it’s an attempt to give people a language for talking about something really hard to talk about — how we love.

The quiz will tell you which of five types of love you are. The first is words of affirmation, which is when you express your feelings for another through spoken or written appreciation. The second is acts of service, which is when you do things for others. The third is touch, which is when you express love through physical intimacy. The fourth is quality time, which is when you spend meaningful time with others. The fifth is gift giving, which is when you express your love through giving.

The way we give and receive affection is deeply rooted in our values and the relationships we’re in. But when I think about the ways I love, it’s really hard for me to make the process so simple. I’m sure I have been giving and receiving love in these five ways. But for example, an act of service, like picking up food for someone else, could mean so many different things in different contexts. It could be a sign of affection, or it could be a way to exude power by doing something for them. Quality time could be a sign of affection, or it could be a way to extract intimacy from someone so you can tell your friends about it later.

I think it’s hard to talk about love because it’s so context-dependent. When I think back to the ways I’ve loved, there are so many things that come to mind. One way I’ve expressed my love is by taking my time with something. When I was in college, I would often take on the role of problem-solving for my friends. There was a point where my girlfriend was feeling really lonely, and the best way I could think to be there for her was to take a few days to give her my undivided attention. So I flew to her and spent a few days with her, showing her that I cared. That was my way of loving her, by giving her my time.

I think the way we love is so specific to the person we are loving and the relationship we are in. It’s hard to have a language for love that works for everyone. But I think it’s important to try to talk about it, even if it’s hard. And I think the love language quiz is a good place to start, even if it doesn’t capture the full complexity of love.
