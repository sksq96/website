Maybe I should give up on meditation.

I started meditating because I thought it would make me less of a dick.  This is still my main attraction to it.  But we don’t know that it does that.  There are some studies that suggest it might.  But the studies only look at short term effects, and the outcomes are strictly limited to what questionnaires can measure.  They can’t directly measure how much of a dick I am.  I can’t directly measure how much of a dick I am.  And even if I could measure it, there’s no guarantee that changes to my current level of dickishness would generalize to all of the situations I encounter.  So the only way I’ll ever know if it works, is to do it and see what happens.

But how do I know that doing it is working?  The only way to really know is if I’m less of a dick.  But I’m not sure if I’m less of a dick or not.

So I came up with some proxies that I think might indicate less dickishness.  One of the proxies I used was being able to meditate for longer periods of time.  If I can meditate for longer periods of time, I should be better at controlling my thoughts, which should make me better able to catch myself when I’m being a dick.  Another proxy I used was the number of thoughts I had per minute.  If I had fewer thoughts per minute, I should be better able to control my thoughts, which should make me better able to catch myself when I’m being a dick.

I tracked these things over time.  I was able to meditate for longer periods of time.  And I had fewer thoughts per minute.  But I’m not sure if I’m less of a dick or not.  There could be other factors.  Maybe I’m not practicing enough to make a difference.  Maybe the proxies aren’t actually correlated to how big of a dick I am.  Maybe I wasn’t paying enough attention to how much of a dick I was being in the first place.  Or maybe I’m just not a very big dick to begin with, so there’s not much room for improvement.

The thing is, I don’t even care about the meditation stuff in the first place.  I care about being less of a dick.  But I can’t tell if I’m being less of a dick or not.  So I don’t know if I should keep meditating or give up.
