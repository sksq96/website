Holy fuck I’ve been looking for acid for a long time. First time during the pandemic in a year!

And I had one magnificent ride.

I found this guy who named himself Santa that was offering everyone 1 free tab for the Bicycle Day. It came in my mailbox, in an envelope. There were these 10 small things. And I got to believe stupidly that 1 tab means 1ug (apparently that wasn’t the case).

I went slow. I had 1. Then I had 2. In the afternoon I was like, fuck it, let’s have a quote unquote normal dose. It was 1000ug.

I’ve never seen Dementors, from Harry Potter. But I saw 3 of them in my room and they were taking over my emotional space. It felt like the Joker from the movie, I’m forced to smile. Eventually everything became animated, it was fucking crazy. Everything felt like I’m in an animated Disney movie.

And I went to ER. There were cops. I was scared my girlfriend was scared. I was sure this is the place you are sent when you do acid. And you’ll be here for eternity (I’m sure my mind made me believe I’ll be in the ER forever. Being stuck for eternity scared the shit out of me). My belief landscape was about to be squashed when the doc came in and gave me a sedative. Oh, these beautiful trippy visuals! Why didn’t I actually believed before tripping that your actual reality could be different. For good, or for the bad.

Well to be really honest, I’ve been preparing for a psychedelic journey for about 2 months. I’ve read “How to change your mind”. I’ve read neuroscience research in the effect on the plasticity of the brain. (highly recommended REBUS: RELAXED BELIEFS UNDER PSYCHEDELICS AND THE ANARCHIC BRAIN) Unfortunately yes, you are right. When the time came, stupid me, didn’t researched the very basic thing of what’s the dosage of 1tab.

But the journey was worth for it.
