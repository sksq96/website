[I started this repository](https://github.com/sksq96/nuggets) on 2022-10-11. The short version of why: nuggets

Here's the honest version, with the parts I usually leave out.

I did this as a challenge for myself to accept small pull requests. I figured I would try out 100 PRs in 100 days. No matter how small.

The repository is for personal use. I just wanted to keep track of stuff I came across that were too small to write a blog post about but still wanted to be documented.

* * *

That's the short version. Below is the long one, and it's a little messier on purpose.

### Context

I've always loved learning new things. But there have been times where my notes get out of hand as I can easily create a new note.

There are times where I'm proud of my notes and I could easily share them with others, but there are times where I just felt like my notes were too messy to share with others.

I was watching [Shawn Wang's](https://www.swyx.io/) talk on [Learn in Public](https://www.swyx.io/writing-small), and part of the talk was about sharing the small things you come across, there were some examples he gave that were like this, but I can't find it right now.

### The Why

I generally don't like messy things, but I have accepted that I will always have messy things in my life. It's just a matter of how much I can tolerate.

Most of my notes will be messy, but I decided to create this repository to store the small things I come across that I want to share with others.

### Good examples of small things

- Random commands I found useful
- Snippets of code that I think are interesting
- Short explanations of concepts that I think are interesting

### Bad examples of small things

- Full blown projects
- Large implementations of code
- Long explanations of concepts

I am doing this as a challenge for myself to be comfortable with accepting pull requests that are very small. I think that making small pull requests with small changes is a useful skill that I don't have yet.
