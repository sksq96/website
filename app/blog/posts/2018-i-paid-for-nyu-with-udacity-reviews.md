I'm writing this from a cozy little apartment in Brooklyn. I moved here two weeks back. I know I'm a bit late to writing my first semester blog, but better late than never.

First, how did I get here?

I'm funding my education through my savings over the last few years from a side job. I started to review projects for Udacity in the early 2017 and started to do quite a bit of it as I developed an expertise in it.

I review Nanodegree projects. The nanodegree program is a pretty rigorous program in which Udacity offers courses on various topics like Web Development, Deep Learning, Self Driving Cars. As a part of this program, students have to do projects. These projects are reviewed by subject matter experts called reviewers. Each time a student submits a project, it goes into review. The reviewer evaluates the project against a rubric and returns it to the student with feedback on how to improve it. Each time the student submits their projects, the reviewer is paid. For example, there are 3 projects in the Deep Learning Nanodegree and hence the reviewer is paid 3 times. This process repeats until the student passes the project.

I started working as a reviewer for a few courses, but then settled for Deep Learning and AI. Recently, I started to review the Self Driving Car Nanodegree.

**What was my first semester like?**

I took 2 courses in this semester.

I took a Deep Learning (DL) course ([link](https://github.com/sksq96/nyu-dl)). The professor (Yann LeCun) was really interesting and had made a lot of contributions to the field. I worked on a research project ([link](https://github.com/sksq96/nyu-dl/tree/master/Assignment%206)) with another student. We ended up see a 0.6% improvement in the accuracy over the baseline. The assignment was really fun.

I also took a Natural Language Processing (NLP) course and worked on a project on Natural Language Inference. I worked on a baseline model and improved over it by 3%. I have written a blog on it [here](https://towardsdatascience.com/natural-language-inference-with-pytorch-7f98c3ca9c54).

The first semester has been really fun. I'm starting my second semester next week and I'm excited to see what we do.

I'm still in awe that I'm writing this blog. I had no idea about Udacity back then. I'm really grateful to Udacity for funding me and providing me with this opportunity. I'm paying for NYU through Udacity reviews. It's pretty crazy to be honest.
