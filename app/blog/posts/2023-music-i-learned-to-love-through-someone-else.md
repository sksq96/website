After reading the title, you probably have a list of songs in mind that you would want to include in this list. I am taking a different route. I am going to write about a song I have already mentioned here, and I’ll write about why I like it so much.

The song is “Like That” by Ansar. My brother introduced me to this song, and he likes the bass drop in the middle. I don’t like the bass drop. I like how the song builds up to the first drop. The way the violins are playing and the way the drums are pounding. It gets to you when you listen. It makes you want to move. It makes you want to dance.

This post is not about the song. It is about the person who introduced me to it. It is about our relationship. When I hear this song, I think about my brother. I think about the time we spent together. I think about the times we had fun together. I think about the times we had a good laugh. I think about the times we had a good cry. I think about the times we had a good argument.

I am not the kind of person who makes an effort to be in touch with people. If someone wants to be in touch with me, they’ll have to make an effort. It is strange how my brother has been able to make me do that. And it is strange how we’ve gotten closer. We spent a day together, and we saw this comedy show, and we had a good time. We did not talk too much. But it was more than enough to make me realise that I am glad that we are getting closer. And that it was an injustice to my brother that I did not spend more time with him.

I am glad that I got to spend some time with him. And I am glad that he introduced me to this song.

The original title of this post was “Music I love because of a person”. And I think it is a beautiful title. But if I were to name the person I love because of a piece of music, it would be my brother for this song. And I think that is kind of strange. And I kind of like it.
